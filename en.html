TeseoTarot Privacy Policy

Teseo (hereinafter referred to as the 'Company' or 'Developer') values users' personal information and complies with the "Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization and Information Protection" and the "Personal Information Protection Act". This Privacy Policy explains the information collected when users use TeseoTarot (hereinafter referred to as the 'App'), the purpose of its use, and the protective measures taken.

1. Items of Personal Information Collected and Method of Collection This App does not have a membership registration process and does not collect or store sensitive personal information that can identify the user (name, phone number, resident registration number, etc.) on its servers. However, to provide app features and improve service quality, the following data may be stored internally on the device or automatically collected by external services. Data stored internally on the device: Information directly entered by the user, such as Manseryeok (Four Pillars of Destiny) data, is securely stored only within the user's smartphone device (SQLite DB) and is not transmitted to external servers. Automatically collected data (Third-party services): App usage history, device information, advertising identifiers (ADID), etc., may be collected through the third-party services listed below for statistical analysis and advertising purposes.

2. Purpose of Processing Personal Information The App processes minimum information for the following purposes: Service provision: Providing TeseoTarot fortune-telling information, saving records, and providing statistics (processed internally on the device). Service improvement and statistical analysis: Checking app errors and improving usability using Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. Provision of personalized advertisements: Displaying personalized banner ads through Google AdMob.

3. Provision and Entrustment of Personal Information to Third Parties This App does not arbitrarily provide user data to third parties. However, for service operation (statistics and advertising), the following external SDKs (Software Development Kits) are used, and these companies collect and process data according to their own privacy policies. Google AdMob (Personalized Ads): Google Privacy Policy Firebase Analytics (App Statistics and Analysis): Firebase Privacy Policy

4. Procedure and Method of Destroying Personal Information Data stored internally on the device (Manseryeok information; date and time of birth, etc.) is permanently destroyed immediately when the user uninstalls (removes) the App from their smartphone or clears the App's data in the device settings. Since recovery is impossible, please be careful when deleting data.

5. Protection of Children's Personal Information This App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 14. If the Developer becomes aware that personal information of a child under the age of 14 has been collected, reasonable steps will be taken to delete such information. 6. Chief Privacy Officer and Contact Information If you have any questions regarding privacy protection while using the App, please contact us at the following information. Email: hesu6@naver.com

7. Changes to the Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be modified according to changes in laws or app features. If there are any changes to the content, we will notify you through announcements within the App or update notices in the app market.
